This is an unofficial fansite of the Android: Netrunner game. All rights go to Fantasy Flight Games and their respective artists.

Stimhack is the top Netrunner strategy site online, containing articles, tournament decklists, gameplay videos, a huge forum and more for the benefit of the Netrunner community.

If you want to contribute in any way, by writing articles, creating videos, submitting podcasts or anything else, please let us know! We are always looking for more contributors.

In addition to content creation, Stimhack is directly funded through the support of our amazing community members. Running the site and forums costs a significant amount of credits, and your support will go directly to running special tournaments, and improving the site. The best way to support Stimhack is through our Patreon campaign.

Special thanks to all of our Patreon Patrons!

Winner Commentary

“Pretty standard Gabe, went with Parasites over Knight because I’m dumb like that, they’re slightly better against Jinteki RP and small ICE fast advance decks but I’ll definitely be trying it out with Knights instead in the future. Also needs a Plascrete, Scorched went away so Plascrete did and now that Scorched is back its hard to play with just one.”

“Standard HB fast advance stuff, I switched to this over Jinteki and New Earth Hub because I figured people would be prepared for those decks. Turns out I was right, this deck didn’t drop a game all tourney; the NEXT ICE basically carrying the deck. I was lucky not to encounter too many Parasites but even if I did the rest of the ICE was efficient enough to keep the game going. I didnt notice the lack of sentries until I started playing, definitely need to replace Victor with another Ichi or Rototurret.”

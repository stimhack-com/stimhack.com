3) SanSan!

6) Hunter because I think I need cheap ice. (Why would you pick either Chimera or Hourglass?). Alternately Edge of World is tempting.

8) If I have enough Agenda pts at this point, then Pad Campaign. I took that Braintrust already, so if I need more agendas, Fetal. Need enough agenda points for the deck. Fetal I dont want to score, but I’d rather the runner score it than anything else. Some fetal damage might allow me to make a Sea Source/Scorch kill at some point with the 1 scorch.

9) Encryption Protocol.

10) Edge of World.

11) Dedicated Server probably. I’d pretty much only ever take an Uroboros in Replicating. It costs 6 and delivers no pain.

12) Snare. Works with my Scorch.

13) I’ll take ChiLo for this deck and see how it works. I’ve got a number of traces.

15) Experiential Data. Upgrades arent ever bad since they bluff.

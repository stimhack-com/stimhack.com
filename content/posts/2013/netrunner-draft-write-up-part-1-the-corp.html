Ok, here is what I would pick, given those packs:

1) Nisei MK II. My second choice would be Beta Test, my third would be Archer, and after that I’m torn between Adonis Campaign and Neural Katana. Clearly I’m not valuing Neural Katana as high as these guys. We’ve all only drafted a couple times, hard to tell who is correct here, or if any of us are.

2) Project Vitruvius. My highest priority in draft is getting good agendas. 3/2s and ones with strong powers like Nisei. I figure that if I have an inferior ice or whatever thats not too terrible, its just a card, but an inferior agenda that I have trouble scoring is a problem. The Braintrust and the popup windows were tempting though, but with three popup in the pack I figure one will return.

3) Breaking News. Its awesome.

4) Chum. Is very efficient. And I’ve found in drafting that you end up with some bad ice, and generally some tracer ice thats questionable since you probably cant Scorch them to death or anything. But Chum in front of one of those ice is pretty great.

5) Nisei Mk II again. But lets pretend that I took it pack 1 so it isnt there. I’d take Rototurret or Caduceus.

6) Popup Window.

7) Archived memories or Wall of Static. I guess Archive, since its early and I can go for combos like Scorch with it. Archiving things like Trick of Light is also amazing, or Melange/Adonis/SanSan, etc.

8) Caduceus or Pad Campaign. Pad if I have an identity thats good with it, like Replicating Perfection. Caduceus for sure if I have NBN.

9) Scorched Earth. Its still here late, might as well add bite to my tags. I have an Archived already, which you really want for the scorch deck. And I took breaking news earlier. I find that I tend to play a number of mid to late pick tracer ice, because thats whats left in the draft.

10) Popup Window!

11) Green Level. I’d take Janus if I had gotten a Beta Test or Oversight AI earlier. Might consider it anyway hoping to get more of those later.

12) Draco if I’m NBN. Otherwise Heimdall.

13) Caduceus, since I cant take that scorch because I actually already took it pick 9.

14) Bleah. Uroboros if I’m Replicating Perfection. Otherwise Mandatory.

15) Ash, or Precognition, hoping to get Beta Tests later.

Recently Sneakysly(Anthony Giovannetti) and I (Anthony Roybal) decided to do a Netrunner draft (as seen here) and record our picks and the thought process behind them. We started off with an identity draft that went like this.

Player 1 Pick 1: Haas Bioroid: Engineering the Future

Player 2 Pick 2 (Me) : Gabriel Santiago

Player 3 Pick 3 (SneakySly): Kate “Mac” McCaffrey

Player 4 Pick 4: Weyland: Because we Build it

Player 4 Pick 5: Chaos Theory

Player 3 Pick 6 (SneakySly): Jinteki: Personal Evolution

Player 2 Pick 7 (Again Me): Jinteki Replicating Perfection

Player 1 Pick 8: Whizzard

I believe that, assuming no influence restrictions, this is just about the correct draft order with a couple spots switched perhaps. We end up with Gabriel who is very strong and Jinteki: RP which is pretty middle of the pack for corps but at least has a powerful effect. Knowing that we have Jinteki RP makes me want to draft very nasty ice that the runner won’t want to run into and monetary assets during the corporation draft. Whereas drafting Gabe makes me want to focus on a resource denial runner deck. With these things in mind we start the corporation draft first.

Below is a log of how my corporate draft went. By default it will show what SneakySly thinks of the pack. To see what I ended up picking and why, click “Display Anthony’s Pick and Analysis”. Enjoy, and let us know what you think in the comments below.

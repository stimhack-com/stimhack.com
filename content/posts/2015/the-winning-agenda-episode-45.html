On our agenda:‘Old Hollywood’ Card Highlight!

– Old Hollywood Runner and Corp Cards! What do they do? Do they do things? Lets find out! This week the panel reviews the penultimate pack of the SanSan cycle. From the seemingly useless jank like Fan Site and Ronald Five, to the more interesting or competitive cards like Film Critic and Vanity Project. This is an episode you won’t want to miss!

Please note, due to technical errors, Panelist Wilfy Horig is only present for some of the recording of this episode.

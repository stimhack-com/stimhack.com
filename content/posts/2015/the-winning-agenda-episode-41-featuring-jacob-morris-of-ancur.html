On our agenda:

Special Guest Jacob Morris, the Webmaster of the Android: Netrunner Comprehensive Unofficial Rules!

This week we interview Jacob Morris, the hero behind Project ANCUR. Jacob explains what ANCUR is, its inception, and his long term goals for not just the website, but the Netrunner community. Host Brian Holland, together with Judge Tom Daniel and Panelist Hollis Eacho, discuss the frequency of FAQ releases and the matter in which new rulings are released. Jacob also touches on the importance of the Judge Program and the upcoming floor rules. Stay tuned for this excellent, behind-the-scenes tour of one of the most important community-driven aspects of modern Netrunner!

On our agenda:

2015 Australian Nationals recap!

  • This week the Panelists recap on their individual performances at Nationals 2015! They reflect on the choices they made both in and out of the tournament, and discuss what they learned from the process and which of them are better for it.

Nationals Top 16

  • Panelist Wilfy Horig and special guest Alex Forndran give us the inside info on their Top 16 games. Which they won, which they lost, what they expected and what their outcome was: In Alex’s case, carrying him all the way to the final!

The Official TWA Team Event

  • Panelist Liam Prasad takes us behind the scenes of the first TWA team event that was held on Nationals weekend. Shielsy discusses both the changes he had to make to regular tournament structure, and the overall result of the event. This is not to miss if you’re looking to run a team event!

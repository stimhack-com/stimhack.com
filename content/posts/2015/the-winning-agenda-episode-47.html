On our agenda:

Special Guest US Nationals Top 16 Competitor Asher Stuhlman!

– Our guest this week recaps on his experience as a Top 16 competitor for the 2015 US Nationals! Asher discusses his deck choices, the preparation leading up to the tournament and of course the event itself! We also take some time to discuss what Asher and his Chicago-based play group have been brewing up in the way of competitive decks with the recently available Data and Destiny!

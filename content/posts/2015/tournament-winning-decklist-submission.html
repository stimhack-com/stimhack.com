The Tournament Winning Decklist page has been updated with new functionality to better scale for the future of Netrunner. Now players can submit winning decklists themselves, and after approval they will automatically be added to the main winning decklists page in a single searchable and easy to navigate table for everyone to see.

The winning decklists section will continue to see improvements and refinement going forward thanks to the help of lpoulter who has been amazingly badass at working with us, and you can expect to see ongoing tweaks coming soon.

Please take a look to see if a tournament that you won is not on our list, and if so submit it and let others know about your victory! If you have any thoughts or recommendations you would like to see, please let us know.

HEY MA! LOOK! – WE ARE ON STIMHACK NOW!

Whats up Stimhackers // Hacktivists

Hosts this week – Spags // Hollis // Jamieson
In this episode we look at the kick off to Regional Season. We talk about what we are seeing and how the cards from the SANSAN Cycle are contributing. Hollis and Spags have already played in local Regional’s with Spags taking home some hardware and a Playmat to hang on the fridge. We discuss what’s hot and what you should be looking out for when you play your tournament. Add in some deck building tips and our top ID’s. We cover it all. So glad you could join us.
